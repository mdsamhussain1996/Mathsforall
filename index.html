Gradient Descent Optimizer

Gradient descent finds the minimum of a cost function by iteratively moving in the direction of the steepest negative gradient.

w ← w − α · ∇L(w)

where α is the learning rate and ∇L is the gradient of the loss function.

Learning rate α: 0.15
